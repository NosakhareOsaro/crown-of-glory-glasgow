Before you arrive

What to expect

Parking & arrival

Street parking is available nearby, and our welcome team will be at the door to point you in the right direction — just look for a friendly face in a gold lanyard.

The service

Sundays run about two hours, with lively worship, prayer, and a practical message. Dress however you're comfortable — many come straight from a lie-in.

Kids' provision

Children's Church runs alongside the main service for ages 0–12, with safer-recruitment-checked leaders. Just sign in at the children's desk when you arrive.

Connect afterwards

Stay for tea, coffee, and a proper conversation after the service — this is honestly where most friendships at Crown of Glory begin.

Good questions

Frequently asked questions

Where do I park?

There's metered street parking around our West End venue, and a public car park a short walk away. Our welcome team can point you to the nearest option when you arrive.

Is there a kids' programme?

Yes — Children's Church runs every Sunday for ages 0–12, split by age group, with games, worship, and a Bible story at their level. Sign-in takes a minute at the children's desk.

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in. You'll see everything from suits to jeans in the room — come as you are, not as you think you should be.

How long is the service?

Around two hours, including worship, prayer, notices, and the message. There's no pressure to stay for all of it on your first visit — do what's comfortable for you.
