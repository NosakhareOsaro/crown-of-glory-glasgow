Our story

A family church, from day one

Crown of Glory Glasgow began small — a handful of families who wanted a church where nobody was a stranger for long. We started meeting in a borrowed room, sharing chairs and songbooks, and that closeness never left us, even as the room got fuller.

We're part of the Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG), but the heart of what we do is simple: worship that's honest, teaching that's practical, and a family that shows up for each other — at hospital bedsides, at graduations, and every Sunday in between.

Today we gather in a rented venue in Glasgow's West End. The building isn't ours yet, but the welcome always has been.

What we hold to

Our mandate & beliefs

Our mandate

We exist to make heaven — to see lives transformed by the gospel and to raise a family of believers who reflect God's glory in Glasgow and beyond. We take seriously the call to reach our city, one relationship at a time.

The Scriptures

We believe the Bible is God's word, fully trustworthy, and the final authority for what we believe and how we live.

God, Father, Son, and Spirit

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — who created us, redeemed us, and lives within us.

Salvation by grace

We believe salvation is a free gift, received by faith in Jesus Christ's death and resurrection, not earned by our own effort.

The church as family

We believe the church is meant to function as a family — encouraging, correcting, and carrying one another, not just a building we visit on Sundays.

A life of prayer

We believe prayer is at the centre of a healthy life and a healthy church, and we make space for it in everything we do together.
