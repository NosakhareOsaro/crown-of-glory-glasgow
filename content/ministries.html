Get connected — our ministries

Every ministry here is really just a smaller family inside the bigger one. Find the team that fits your gifts, your season, or simply your curiosity.

Choir & Music

Lead the church in worship through singing and instruments — no professional experience required, just a willing heart.

Rehearsals: Thursdays, 7pm

Ushering

Be the first warm face people meet — greeting, seating, and helping the service run smoothly from the door in.

Team meets: Sundays, 9:45am

Media

Sound, livestream, and photography — help us share what happens in the room with those who can't be there.

Training: monthly, dates announced

Children's Church

Teach and care for our youngest members with games, songs, and Bible stories at their level. DBS checks required.

Serves: Sundays, 10:30am

Youth

Walking alongside our teenagers through faith, friendship, and the ordinary chaos of growing up.

Meets: Fridays, 6pm (term-time)

Welfare

Practical care for church members going through a hard season — meals, visits, and a listening ear.

On call: year-round

Prayer Band

Standing in the gap for the church, our city, and every request that comes through our prayer line.

Meets: Fridays, 7pm

Men's & Women's Fellowship

Focused community and discipleship in smaller groups — honest conversation, prayer, and real friendship.

Meets: monthly, alternating Saturdays
